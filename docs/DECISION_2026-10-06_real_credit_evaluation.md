## 4. Structural

SPY $1-wide at $10K is not killed by the bid/ask (penny-wide legs, about $1/ct friction) but by geometry: strikes that avoid a touch (≥0.5% OTM) paid ≥$10/ct in 6.5% of rows (1.7% at ≥0.6%); strikes that pay (0.3 to 0.4%) are touched 36 to 51% of days, and a touched $1-wide costs $30 to $45/ct against $10 collected. XSP or SPX with OPRA quotes fixes data and fills, not geometry; one SPX $10-wide is $1,000 max loss, 10% of this account, so 1 to 2% risk sizing implies $50K to $100K. Capital cannot buy an undemonstrated edge; prove it on SPY paper first.

### friction-scale
At $10K on SPY the bid/ask is not the enemy: SPY 0DTE legs are penny-wide and entry friction is ~$1/ct flat. What kills small credits is the fixed fee and the exit. If the bot holds to expiry, any executable credit >= $11/ct keeps friction+fees under 20% and the market offered that at >=0.3% OTM in 74% of 5-min rows, essentially every row before 13:00 ET (01:00 SGT). If the bot takes a 40% TP (buying back a now-cheap, now-wider leg), the bar is ~$30/ct and the market offered it only in the morning (0 rows after 13:00 ET). Config F's two constraints are mutually exclusive on this tape: at the >=0.6% cushion with $1 wings, >= $10/ct appeared in 3.9% of rows and >= $15 never; the >=$11 hold-to-expiry bar at >=0.6% appeared in 24.6% of rows, all before 13:00 ET. A 'silently, slowly' bot therefore needs, in this order: enter 09:30-12:00 ET (21:30-00:00 SGT) only; prefer $5-wide (or $2-wide) wings, which pay >=$10 at >=0.6% in 34% of rows versus 3.9% for $1-wide; hold to expiry or stop-only rather than a 40% TP that re-pays fees and exit spread; and set the executable floor at $11-15/ct, not $10. SPX cannot be shown to be cheaper in friction without SPX quotes, and at $10K one SPX $10-wide is 10% of the account per spread, so it is a sizing problem before a friction problem. Alpaca paper's $0 commission flatters every one of these numbers; use the $0.65-leg case for any go/no-go.

### vrp-time-of-day
No time-of-day window shows a statistically supportable variance risk premium in these 6 sessions. The open (09:30-10:00 ET / 21:30-22:00 SGT) is the only bucket where implied exceeded realized on the mean (+0.06% of spot, ~+$48 per straddle contract, 4 sessions, t≈0.8), and every other bucket was net negative for the seller because one trend day (09-30) outran the straddle in every bucket and two sessions supply two-thirds of the rows. The 3-year SPX history does not support the open as the seller's window: per unit of remaining √time, the first 30 and last 30 minutes are the highest-movement windows and 12:00-14:00 ET (00:00-02:00 SGT) is persistently the lowest (250d); the last 20 sessions in the file (Aug 10-Sep 4) realized only ~half the 250d norm, and the live Sep 8-Oct 5 tape is 0.67-0.93× norm.
